What this page does and does not claim

  • Our two models run live in your browser through ONNX Runtime Web. Their output matches the published Python packages - the same class at every tested position - and CI checks exactly that.
  • Mishkala and FLAN-T5 outputs on the built-in samples are precomputed. They cannot run on a static page, and they are labelled as such wherever they appear.
  • Leaderboard numbers are quoted from the published SadeedDiac-25 table, produced by that benchmark’s own letter-level evaluator. They are never mixed with ours.
  • Every number says which evaluator produced it. The Board’s measured here rows come from the benchmark’s own letter-level evaluator, scored on all 1200 rows. The Score tab runs a different, self-contained scorer inside this page — letter-level DER over marked letters, word-level WER — and says so on the tab. Nothing is placed in a column it does not belong to.
